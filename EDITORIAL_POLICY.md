# Editorial policy

Version 1.0 — 3 September 2026

Astar Tools is deliberately curated. Inclusion means a tool is relevant to building useful AI systems; it does not automatically mean Astar uses, endorses or recommends it.

Every addition requires explicit Astar approval before it is merged or published. Suggestions and pull requests are proposals, not acceptance.

## Selection criteria

An entry should:

- solve a concrete problem in the AI-system lifecycle;
- have primary documentation or source material available for review;
- add something distinct rather than pad a category; and
- state commercial, licensing and operational constraints plainly.

## Verification rules

- Prefer the project’s official documentation, canonical repository and licence text.
- Record a review date per entry.
- Keep missing or ambiguous information as `unknown`.
- Distinguish a hosted service from an open-source client, SDK or command-line tool.
- Do not infer self-hosting from source availability alone; check whether a usable deployment is documented.
- Avoid affiliate links, paid placement and ranking language.

## Corrections and removals

Corrections should cite a primary source and identify the exact field that changed. An entry may be removed if the project becomes unsafe, misleading, abandoned, redundant, or no longer relevant to the collection.

Material changes are reviewed through pull requests so the editorial history remains inspectable.
